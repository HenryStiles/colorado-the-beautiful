Mount Bierstadt

"Standing on the summit of Mount Bierstadt near Idaho Springs, Colorado, I was surrounded by breathtaking views of the Rocky Mountains, alpine tundra, and miles of natural landscape stretching in every direction. At 14,065 feet, Mount Bierstadt is one of Colorado’s most popular fourteeners and offers a remarkable perspective of the state’s rugged beauty. This location is especially meaningful to me because it was the first fourteener I climbed during the summer I turned 14, making it both a memorable adventure and a personal milestone."

Shared By Tatum Sneed
Location Idaho Springs, CO
